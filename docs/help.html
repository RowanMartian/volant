Everything in Volant is in its menus too, so if you forget a shortcut, it's one click away.

Questions

Tabs and pages

Where did my tabs go when I hid the sidebar?

They're still open. Press ⌘S, or click the sidebar button next to the window controls, to bring it back.

Why does a tab reload when I go back to it?

It was asleep. Tabs you haven't looked at in a while give their memory back and reload when you return. Change how long that takes, or turn it off, in Settings → Sleep background tabs.

How do I add sites to the home page?

Click the pencil in the bottom-right corner of the home page. Add sites, drag them into order, and turn the weather widget on or off from the same panel.

Blocking

A site doesn't work properly.

Click the shield in the address bar to turn blocking off for just that site. The page reloads without any blocking. Click it again to turn blocking back on.

An ad got through.

Press ⌥⌘B, point at it and click Block. It's saved to My filters. If several ads get through on a site, open Blocker Settings and choose Update Now to fetch the latest lists.

What does "Powered by uBlock" mean?

Volant's blocker uses uBlock Origin's filter lists and filter syntax, compiled into WebKit's built-in content blocker. More about blocking.

Updates and data

How do I update Volant?

When an update is ready, Update available shows at the bottom of the sidebar. Click it. If Volant can't write to the folder it's in, it opens the download page instead.

Where are my settings and history?

In plain text files in ~/Library/Application Support/Volant. Downloads go to your Downloads folder.

How do I make Volant my default browser?

Choose Volant → Make Volant Your Default Browser, or set it in Settings.

Something else?

Open an issue on GitHub and describe what happened. Volant's version is in Settings.
