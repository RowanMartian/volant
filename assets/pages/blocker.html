One filter per line, in uBlock Origin's syntax: example.com##.banner hides an element, ||ads.example^ blocks a server, @@||site.example^ makes an exception, example.com##+js(set, adsEnabled, false) runs a scriptlet. Lines starting with ! are comments.

Blocking is off on these sites, one per line. The shield in the address bar adds or removes the site you're on.
