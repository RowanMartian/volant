Privacy

Nothing leaves your Mac but the pages you visit.

Volant has no accounts, no sync, no analytics and no telemetry. There's no Volant server to send anything to.

{{icon:lock}}What stays on your Mac

In plain text, in ~/Library/Application Support/Volant.

  • SettingsYour choices, in settings.txt
  • Open tabsSo they come back at launch
  • HistoryClear it any time in Settings or History
  • FavouritesThe sites on your home page
  • BlockerFilter lists, My filters and trusted sites
Cookies, caches and site data are kept by WebKit, as they are for Safari. Clear them per site or all at once in Settings.

{{icon:eye}}What Volant asks the internet for

Besides the pages you open, only these.

  • UpdatesGitHub's release page for Volant, at launch and every few hours
  • Filter listsEach list from its usual address, every four days, while blocking is on
  • WeatherOpen-Meteo, with only the city you chose, while the widget is on
  • Site iconsThe icon of a page you visit, from that same site
  • SearchWhat you type goes to the search engine you picked, when you search

Blocking protects you by default.

Ad and tracker blocking, powered by uBlock, is on from the first launch. It stops requests to known trackers inside WebKit, before they're sent, and it can stop links reporting your clicks and WebRTC revealing your local IP address.
