Download Volant.

Free and open source. 1.3 MB, for macOS 12 Monterey or later, on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

{{icon:download}}Download Volant.dmg Release notes

Every download is built by GitHub from the public source, after it passes its tests.

Install

Three steps, once.

  1. Open Volant.dmg and drag Volant onto Applications.
  2. Open Volant. The first time, macOS says it can't check the developer, because Volant isn't notarized by Apple. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway.
  3. Set it up. Volant asks for your search engine, whether to block ads and trackers, and whether to make it your default browser. You can change all of it later in Settings.

Prefer the Terminal? This clears the download warning instead of step 2:

xattr -cr /Applications/Volant.app

{{icon:update}}Updates

Volant checks for a new build at launch and every few hours. When there is one, Update available appears at the bottom of the sidebar. Click it, and Volant downloads the update, checks its signature, swaps it in and reopens with your tabs.

Or choose Volant → Check for Updates… at any time.

{{icon:box}}Uninstall

Drag Volant from Applications to the Trash. To remove your settings, tabs and history too, delete this folder:

~/Library/Application Support/Volant
From source

Build it yourself.

On a Mac with Rust and Xcode's command line tools:

git clone {{repo}}
cd volant
bash scripts/bundle-macos.sh
open dist/Volant.app

The engine pieces build and test on any OS with cargo test -p volant-core.
